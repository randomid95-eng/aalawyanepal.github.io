AALAWYA Nepal

About Us

Aalawya Nepal is a collective dedicated to movement lawyering and feminist advocacy. We believe in the power of law as a tool for social change and work to strengthen connections between legal practice and grassroots movements.

What We Do

  • Movement lawering with people in the center using law as a tool for an emancipated and just society
  • Taking a feminist decolonial approach to dismantle unjust sociolegal structures for sustained systemic changes
  • Crossmovement collaborations with existing movements focusing on intersections of historical and continued marginalizations, collective resistance, and healing.
